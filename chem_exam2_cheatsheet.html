1. LEWIS STRUCTURES (4-step recipe)

  1. Count valence e⁻: sum group e⁻; +1 per (−) charge, −1 per (+) charge. (SO₄²⁻ = 6+24+2 = 32)
  2. Skeleton: central = lowest EN / largest / most valences (C, N, B, S, P, Xe, Cl w/ O). H & F always peripheral. Peripheral atoms bond only to central atoms.
  3. Fill outer atoms first → then central; make multiple bonds until octets (H = 2).
  4. Formal charge check: FC = VE − NBE − ½BE (= VE − lone-pair e⁻ − # bonds)

Pick best structure: (a) FCs minimized/zero (S=C=S not C=S=S) (b) no like-sign FC on neighbors (ONOO⁻) (c) (−) FC on the more EN atom (N₂O: N=N=O / N≡N–O) (d) least FC variation. Equivalent options → resonance (NO₃⁻, O₃, CO₃²⁻, benzene).

2nd-row atoms (C,N,O,F) NEVER exceed 8 e⁻. Period ≥3, groups 14–18 can be hypervalent (PCl₅, SF₆, XeF₄, SF₄). Hypovalent: B, Be, Al (BF₃ = 6 e⁻ on B, BeH₂ = 4). Radicals: odd e⁻ count (NO•, NO₂•, O₂⁻•).

Bond order (Lewis) = avg # bonds over resonance structures = total bond pairs ÷ # equivalent bonds: O₃ = 1.5, NO₃⁻ = 4/3, CO₃²⁻ = 4/3, NO₂⁻ = 1.5, SO₄²⁻ (octet) = 1. Higher BO → shorter & stronger bond.

4. VALENCE BOND THEORY & HYBRIDIZATION (5.1–5.3)

Bond = overlap of 2 half-filled orbitals (same phase/color), 1 e⁻ each → pair (Pauli). More overlap = stronger bond. Opposite phase → node → antibonding. Optimum bond length = overlap gain vs. nuclear repulsion.

σ bond: head-on, along bond axis, cylindrical (s–s, s–p, p–p end-on, hybrid–hybrid/–s). π bond: sideways p–p, density above/below axis, weaker than σ, nodal plane on axis.

Single = 1σ · Double = 1σ+1π · Triple = 1σ+2π (π's are perpendicular: pₓ–pₓ and p_y–p_y)

5. MO THEORY (5.4) — LCAO

MOs spread over whole molecule. n AOs → n MOs. Bonding (constructive, +, e⁻ between nuclei, lower E); antibonding (*) (destructive, extra node, higher E). Antibonding raised MORE than bonding lowered → He₂, Be₂, Ne₂ don't exist. g = gerade (symmetric), u = ungerade.

BO = ½ (bonding e⁻ − antibonding e⁻)

Order — Li₂…N₂ (s–p mixing): σg2s < σ*u2s < πu2p (×2) < σg2p < π*g2p (×2) < σ*u2p
Order — O₂, F₂, Ne₂: σg2s < σ*u2s < σg2p < πu2p (×2) < π*g2p (×2) < σ*u2p

Fill bottom-up, Hund's rule in degenerate π's. Unpaired e⁻ → paramagnetic; all paired → diamagnetic. Total spin S = ½ × (# unpaired).

Heteronuclear (HF, NO, CO): more EN atom's AOs are LOWER in energy; bonding MO looks more like (closer in E to) the EN atom's AO → e⁻ pulled toward it. AOs that don't overlap = non-bonding MOs (= lone pairs). Big energy mismatch → ionic.

VB vs MO: VB = localized 2-center bonds + LPs (basis of Lewis); MO = delocalized, includes antibonding, explains O₂ paramagnetism & fractional BO.

  • Central atoms: H₂O→O, NCl₃→N, SO₂→S, HCN→C, HClO₃→Cl, XeF₄→Xe, PCl₅→P. H: 1 bond, O: 2, N: 3, C: 4, B: 3.

Acid/Base (Brønsted–Lowry)

Acid = H⁺ donor, base = H⁺ acceptor; acid → conjugate base, base → conjugate acid. Arrhenius: ↑H₃O⁺ / ↑OH⁻ in water. Amphoteric: H₂O, HCO₃⁻, HSO₄⁻, HPO₄²⁻, [Mg(OH₂)₅(OH)]⁺ (CA = [Mg(OH₂)₆]²⁺, CB = [Mg(OH₂)₄(OH)₂]).

Strong acids (100% ionized, sheet): H₂S₂O₇, HI, HBr, HCl, H₂SO₄ (1st H⁺), HClO₄, HNO₃ (+HClO₃). Strong bases: OH⁻, O²⁻, S²⁻, NH₂⁻ (metal hydroxides/oxides). Weak: HF, CH₃COOH, H₃PO₄, H₂CO₃, NH₄⁺, H₂S, HCN / NH₃, CO₃²⁻, CN⁻, F⁻, acetate.

Inventories: strong HA: H₃O⁺, A⁻, H₂O (no HA). Weak HA: HA, A⁻(little), H₃O⁺(little), H₂O. NaOH: Na⁺, OH⁻, H₂O. H₂SO₄(aq): H₃O⁺, HSO₄⁻, H₂O (little SO₄²⁻). K₂O: O²⁻ + H₂O → 2OH⁻ (no O²⁻ in water).

Net ionic: strong+strong: H₃O⁺ + OH⁻ → 2H₂O. Weak acid + OH⁻: CH₃COOH + OH⁻ → CH₃COO⁻ + H₂O (write weak acid intact). Gas-forming: CO₃²⁻ + 2H₃O⁺ → H₂CO₃ + 2H₂O → CO₂(g) + 3H₂O (also S²⁻→H₂S, SO₃²⁻→SO₂).

Acid-strength trends: → across row EN↑ → stronger (CH₄<NH₃<H₂O<HF). ↓ down group size↑ → stronger (HF weakest, HI strongest; lower charge density = stabler anion). More O's → stronger (HNO₃ > HNO₂ > HNO). Nonmetal oxides acidic (SO₃ > SO₂), metal oxides basic.

Redox & oxidation numbers

OIL RIG: Oxidation = Loses e⁻ (ON ↑); Reduction = Gains e⁻ (ON ↓). Oxidizer gets reduced; reducer gets oxidized.

ON rules: element = 0 · monatomic ion = charge · Σ = overall charge · F = −1 · O = −2 (peroxide −1, with F +2) · Group 1 +1, Group 2 +2, Al +3 · H +1 (−1 with metals) · halogens −1 (unless with O). From Lewis: give ALL bond e⁻ to more EN atom (split if equal). ON ≠ formal charge. Ex: NO₃⁻ N=+5; CH₃COOH: CH₃ C = −3, COOH C = +3. Thermite Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Fe + Al₂O₃: Fe +3→0 (oxidizer), Al 0→+3 (reducer). Combustion = redox (C oxidized, O reduced), exothermic ΔH < 0.

12. SAFETY MINUTE (asked on exam!)

Autoclave hazards: scalding high-P steam (~30 psi) burns; superheated liquids boil over/splash if removed too soon; steam corrosion → vessel failure/explosion (annual inspection). PPE: eye protection/face shield, lab coat/apron, heat-insulating gloves + training. Pressurized devices: never open before pressure released (MagLab 2015 death). NFPA diamond: blue = health, red = flammability, yellow = instability/reactivity, white = special (W, OX); 0–4 (4 worst). SOP = procedures, hazards, controls, training documentation. SDS 16 sections (1 ID, 2 hazards, 4 first aid, 5 fire, 7 handling/storage, 8 exposure/PPE, 11 toxicology). Pyrophorics (t-BuLi, Sangji UCLA 2009: no lab coat, syringe at 50 mL scale): syringe <50 mL, cannula ≥50 mL, septa (≤~10 punctures), flame-resistant lab coat. Complacency: stay alert in routine work.

  2. VB description: Lewis → hybridization per atom (SN) → draw xyz axes, bond axis = z → label every bond σ/π + which orbitals overlap (sp–1s, sp–sp, p–p) → sketch overlap.
  3. MO / bond order: count valence e⁻ (adjust charge) → choose ordering (≤N₂ vs O₂/F₂) → fill → BO → para/dia → rank bond lengths (high BO = short).
  4. Molar mass: Σ(atoms × atomic mass) with 4–5 sf; show each term.
  5. Empirical/molecular: 100 g → mol → ÷ smallest → integers → k = 𝓜/𝓜emp.
  6. Limiting/yield: balance → mol → ÷ coef → smallest limits → theo yield → %.
  7. Dilution: c₁V₁ = c₂V₂ + describe flask procedure.
  8. "Explain how to make…": give numbers (g solute, g or mL solvent), glassware, steps; hydrates!
  9. Mixing two solutions: (a) inventory each (b) classify rxn type (c) net ionic + phases + spectators.
  10. Titration: mol titrant → ratio → mol analyte → ÷ V.
  11. Gas: list P,V,n,T with units; convert to atm, L, K; pick law; predict qualitatively first (sanity check).
  12. Safety: 2 specific hazards + 3 specific PPE items.

Sig-fig reminders: ×/÷ → fewest sf; +/− → fewest decimals; exact numbers (coefficients, 273.15 def., 1000 mL/L) unlimited. "500." has 3 sf. Keep extra digits until the end.
