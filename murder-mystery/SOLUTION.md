# The murderer was... **NIDHI.** 🎓🔪

---

## 💀 WHY: The Motive
Nidhi wasn't the perfect pre-med student everyone thought she was.
- She **dropped pre-med** after three weeks and was failing Chem 101 (41/100 on Quiz 3).
- Kaka sent her **$15,000** for tuition. She never paid it. She "invested" it in her friend's sneaker-resale startup, **KickFlip**, which collapsed in three weeks.
- Westbrook sent the **final notice** to Kaka — the letter Nitin saw on the desk: *"Student: N. Bharambe."*

At **8:30 PM**, Kaka confronted her privately: at breakfast he would tell her parents everything, and his **new will** would leave her **nothing** until she told the truth herself.

But under the **old will** — still legal until the new one was signed — Nidhi inherited Kesar Villa and most of the fortune. If Kaka never made it to breakfast, her secret would die with him and she'd get everything.

## 🧪 HOW: The Method
1. **8:45 PM** — Nidhi took **12 digoxin pills** from the bottle in the upstairs bathroom (30 at 5:30 PM → 18 after). Neel saw her come out, pale, stuffing something into her pocket.
2. **8:50 PM** — She folded the pills in a page torn from her **Chem 101 notebook** and crushed them into powder.
3. **10:03 PM** — She hung up on her roommate Priya ("brb, snack"), pulled on her **Westbrook hoodie with the hood up** — knowing Neel wore the identical one all night — and went downstairs.
4. **10:08 PM** — Rekha-bai stepped out to take a call. Nidhi poured the powder into Kaka's kesar milk, threw the paper in the kitchen trash, and grabbed some chips as her cover.
5. **10:14 PM** — Back upstairs. **10:17 PM** — Back on FaceTime like nothing happened.
6. **10:20 PM** — Rekha-bai served the poisoned milk. By **10:30**, Kaka was seeing yellow and green halos — digoxin poisoning. He died just before **10:50**, still trying to write her name: **"N—"**.

## 🙅 Why Everyone Else Was Innocent
- **Nitin** 🍞 — He *was* the man shouting "You'll ruin me!" — about his secret $40,000 food-truck loan. But he left at 9:31, 30 minutes before the milk was even made, and his phone shows cricket videos non-stop until the power cut. Kaka was going to **forgive** his loan.
- **Minal** 🩺 — The nurse with the pills and the last visit looked guilty, but she was only there to confess she's **SpiceSpy**. She was on camera in her cardigan, not a hoodie. Her pill count and her text about "yellow" vision actually helped crack the case. Kaka was going to leave her **the house**.
- **Neel** 🚗 — The muddy sneakers, the open drawer, and the "N—" all pointed at him. But the mud came from trying to buff the Jaguar scratch in the rain, the drawer was where Kaka kept his confiscated phone, and he arrived at 10:49 — **after** the poison was already working. He was too tall to be the hooded figure. Kaka was going to leave him **the Jaguar**.

## 📜 Epilogue
When the police finally arrive at sunrise, they find one last envelope in Vicky Kaka's desk drawer, under where Neel's phone had been. It's addressed in shaky handwriting:

> ***"For Nidhi — to open after breakfast."***

Inside is a check for **$15,000** made out to Westbrook University, and a note:

> *"Beta — I was never going to let you drop out. I only wanted you to stop lying to the people who love you. Tell them the truth, study whatever makes you happy, and pay me back when you're a famous film director. — Your Kaka"*

He was going to forgive her too.
