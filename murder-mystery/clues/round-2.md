# 🔎 ROUND 2 — "The Will and the Witness"
### Read aloud to everyone.

---

*It's past midnight. The storm is still raging. Nobody wants to go to sleep in a house with a murderer in it.*

---

## 🩺 Evidence Card 2-A: Dr. Joshi's Verdict
> *"I've done some tests with my kit. This was* ***not*** *natural.*
> *Vicky was poisoned with* ***digoxin*** *— his own heart medication. In a normal dose, it keeps him alive. In a big dose, it stops the heart. There was a* ***chalky white residue*** *at the bottom of his milk glass. Someone crushed up* ***a lot*** *of his pills and put them in his kesar milk.*
> *Digoxin is bitter, but kesar milk is sweet and strong — he'd barely taste it.*
> *Symptoms start maybe 20 to 60 minutes after a big dose: nausea, confusion, strange vision... then the heart gives out."*

**So the milk was poisoned.** Rekha-bai made it at 10:00 and served it at 10:20. **What happened in between?**

## 🗣️ Evidence Card 2-B: Rekha-bai's Confession
Rekha-bai twists her dupatta nervously.

> *"I... I didn't say this before because I didn't think it mattered. I made the milk at 10:00 and put it on Kaka-saab's tray. Around* ***10:08****, my phone rang — my sister, calling about the flooding. I stepped onto the back porch to take it. I was gone maybe* ***five minutes****.*
> *As I was walking out, someone came into the kitchen behind me. I only saw them from the corner of my eye —* ***a grey Westbrook University sweatshirt, with the hood pulled up.***
> *I assumed it was Neel-baba. He was wearing that sweatshirt at dinner."*

## 🗑️ Evidence Card 2-C: The Kitchen Trash
Someone digs through the kitchen trash can and finds a **folded piece of notebook paper** near the top. When unfolded, it's coated in **fine white powder** in the crease.

The paper is torn from a **spiral notebook**. On it, in blue pen, are chemistry notes:

> *PV = nRT ... ideal gas law ... ask Prof. Alvarez about moles??*

And across the top, in red ink:

> ### **CHEM 101 — Quiz 3 — 41/100 — See me. — Prof. Alvarez**

## 📜 Evidence Card 2-D: The New Will
The papers under Kaka's head are his **new will** — a typed draft, **unsigned**. He was going to sign it at breakfast with Dr. Joshi as a witness.

The spilled milk has soaked through part of it and smeared the ink. Here's what can still be read:

> *I, Vikram Bharambe, being of sound mind and nosy nature, leave:*
>
> *To* ***MINAL*** *— Kesar Villa, my home. She is the only one who ever asked how I was feeling. P.S. — Yes, beta, I know you are* ***SpiceSpy****. Your review was correct. The garam masala HAS gotten stale.*
>
> *To* ***NITIN*** *— I forgive the* ***$40,000 loan*** *in full. May "Nitin's Naughty Naan" rest in peace. Now stop hiding things from your wife.*
>
> *To* ***NEEL*** *— my 1968 Jaguar E-Type,* ***scratch and all.*** *Yes, Neel, I saw the gate camera. Learn to drive first.*
>
> *The remainder of my estate, including the Westbrook tuition fund, goes to* ░░░░░░░░ *~~[milk stain]~~* ░░░░░░░░ *... but* ***only after she tells her parents the truth.***
