# 🔎 ROUND 1 — "The Body in the Study"
### Read aloud to everyone.

---

*The lights flicker back on at 11:20 PM. At 11:30 PM, Rekha-bai's scream echoes through Kesar Villa. The whole family rushes to the study...*

Vicky Kaka is slumped over his desk, his head resting on a pile of papers. His silver cane lies on the floor. Moti the beagle is whimpering under the desk.

Dr. Joshi, Kaka's neighbor and doctor, wades over through the flooded garden in his pajamas and rain boots. He examines the body.

---

## 🧾 Evidence Card 1-A: The Crime Scene
- **No wounds.** No blood, no bruises, no signs of a struggle.
- Kaka's glass of **kesar milk** is tipped over. The milk has spilled across the papers on his desk, soaking them.
- The study **window is closed and locked from the inside.** The only way in or out is the door to the main hallway.
- The desk lamp is still on.
- Kaka's **top desk drawer is hanging open.**

## 👣 Evidence Card 1-B: The Footprints
There are **muddy sneaker prints** on the rug — big ones — leading from the hallway door to the desk... and back out again.

It's been pouring rain all night. Whoever made these had been **outside** recently.

## ✍️ Evidence Card 1-C: The Notepad
Next to Kaka's right hand is his notepad. In shaky handwriting, he had started to write something:

> ### **N—**

The pen trails off the page.

## 🩺 Evidence Card 1-D: Dr. Joshi's First Look
> *"His heart simply stopped. Could be natural — he's 78 with a bad heart. But his pupils look strange, and there's a bitter smell in that milk. I don't like it.*
> *Based on his body temperature, I'd say he died somewhere between* ***10:45 and 11:15 PM****."*

## 🗣️ Evidence Card 1-E: Rekha-bai Speaks
> *"I served Kaka-saab his milk at 10:20, same as every night for thirty years. He was fine then — grumpy, but fine.*
> *But earlier, around* ***9:20****, I was dusting in the hallway and I heard a* ***man*** *shouting in the study:* ***'You'll ruin me!'*** *Then a door slammed."*

## 📱 Evidence Card 1-F: Remember Lunch?
Everyone remembers: at lunch today, Kaka **confiscated Neel's phone** in front of the whole family and said, *"You'll get it back when you explain yourself to your father."*

---

## 🗨️ Now: Alibis & Interrogation
Go around the table. Each person introduces their character and explains **where they were from 9:00 PM to 11:30 PM.**

Then ask each other anything. Some questions to get started:
- Who shouted "You'll ruin me!"?
- Whose muddy sneakers are those? What was in that drawer?
- Who does **"N—"** point to? (Nitin? Nidhi? Neel?)
