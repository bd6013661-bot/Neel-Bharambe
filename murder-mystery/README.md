# 🕯️ Murder at Kesar Villa
### A Bharambe Family Murder Mystery — for 4 players, no host needed

> *A thunderstorm. A locked-down mansion. A rich old uncle who "knows everyone's secrets."
> By midnight he's dead — and every single Bharambe had a reason to want him quiet.*

---

## ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — SPOILER RULES

- **Everyone may read this README.** Read it aloud together at the start.
- **Only read YOUR OWN file** in the `characters/` folder. Reading someone else's ruins the game.
- **Do NOT open `SOLUTION.md`** until everyone has made their final accusation.
- Clue cards in `clues/` are opened **one round at a time**, read aloud to the whole group.

One of you is the murderer. **The murderer knows it.** Everyone else does not.

---

## 🏚️ The Story

Great-Uncle **Vikram "Vicky Kaka" Bharambe**, 78, made a fortune with his spice empire, *Kesar Gold Masalas*. He never married, has no kids, and lives alone in a creaky old mansion called **Kesar Villa** with his loyal housekeeper, **Rekha-bai**, who has worked there for 30 years.

This weekend, Vicky Kaka invited the whole Bharambe family to Kesar Villa to celebrate his birthday.

At dinner on Saturday night, while a huge thunderstorm rolled in, he tapped his glass and made an announcement:

> *"Tomorrow at breakfast, I will read my **new will**. Some of you will be very happy. Some of you will be very, very surprised. I am an old man, but I am not a stupid one — **I know every one of your secrets.** Sleep well."*

Then he laughed, took his cane, and went to his study.

At **11:00 PM** the storm knocked out the power. When the lights came back at **11:20 PM**, Rekha-bai went to bring him his blanket.

At **11:30 PM**, she screamed.

Vicky Kaka was slumped over his desk. Dead. His glass of warm **kesar (saffron) milk** was tipped over beside him.

The roads are flooded. The police can't get there until morning. **The family has until sunrise to figure out which one of them did it.**

## 📋 How to Play (about 60–90 minutes)

### Setup (5 min)
1. Everyone opens **only their own** character file in `characters/` and reads it privately.
2. Optional but *highly* recommended: dress up a little, dim the lights, make some chai. 🍵
3. Grab paper and a pen for notes.

### Round 1 — "The Body in the Study" (15–20 min)
1. Someone reads **`clues/round-1.md`** aloud.
2. Going around the table, each person introduces their character and gives their **alibi** for 9:00–11:30 PM (your file tells you what to say).
3. Interrogate each other! Ask anything.

### Round 2 — "The Will and the Witness" (15–20 min)
1. Someone reads **`clues/round-2.md`** aloud.
2. Check your character file — it tells you what you **must reveal** in Round 2.
3. Interrogate again. Motives start coming out...

### Round 3 — "The Camera Never Lies" (15–20 min)
1. Someone reads **`clues/round-3.md`** aloud.
2. Check your character file for your Round 3 reveals.
3. Final interrogation. Push hard.

### The Accusation (5 min)
1. Everyone secretly writes down: **WHO** did it, **HOW**, and **WHY**.
2. Everyone reveals their answer at the same time — count to three!
3. The murderer must now stand up and either confess... or keep lying until someone opens the solution.
4. Read **`SOLUTION.md`** aloud. Dramatically.

## 📜 The Rules of Truth

- **Innocent players:** You must tell the truth about the murder. You're allowed to dodge, stall, or lie about **your own embarrassing secret** — but only until your character file says you have to reveal it.
- **The murderer:** You may lie about anything. Anything at all. 😈
- If a clue card asks everyone to do something (like show their phone), you have to do it.
- Nobody is allowed to say "just check my file." Talk it out!

## 🏆 Scoring (optional)
- **+3** for correctly naming the murderer
- **+1** for getting the method right
- **+1** for getting the motive right
- **Murderer:** +5 if fewer than half the family catches you
