# 🔒 NITIN BHARAMBE — "The Dad"
### ⛔ FOR NITIN'S EYES ONLY. Everyone else — close this file!

---

## ✅ You are INNOCENT.
You did not kill Vicky Kaka. But you have a secret that makes you look *really* bad, and you'd like to keep it that way for as long as possible.

## 🎭 Who You Are
You're Vicky Kaka's nephew. You work hard, you love your family, and you are *terrible* at hiding stress. Lately you've been "on a lot of work calls." You haven't.

## 🤫 Your Secret
Last year you secretly borrowed **$40,000** from Vicky Kaka to start your dream business: a food truck called **"Nitin's Naughty Naan."**

It lasted four months. The truck caught fire (small fire, big smell). The business died. The money is gone.

**Minal has no idea.** You told her the late nights were "work overtime." Every time she brings up money, you change the subject to cricket.

When Kaka said at dinner *"I know every one of your secrets,"* you nearly choked on your biryani.

## 🕰️ Your Night (the TRUTH)
| Time | What you actually did |
|---|---|
| 7:30 PM | Dinner. Kaka's announcement. You started sweating. |
| **9:14 PM** | You went to Kaka's study to beg for more time on the loan. |
| 9:15–9:30 | It got heated. Kaka waved a stack of papers — his new will — and said: *"Tomorrow, someone at this table loses everything. And it is not who you think."* You were SURE he meant you. You shouted **"You'll ruin me!"** and stormed out. |
| 9:31 PM | Walked back down the hallway, rubbing your face, upset. |

## 👀 Something You Saw
While you were in the study at 9:15, you noticed a letter sitting on Kaka's desk, on top of the will:

> **WESTBROOK UNIVERSITY — BURSAR'S OFFICE**
> **FINAL NOTICE: Tuition balance unpaid — $15,000**
> **Student: N. Bharambe**

It seemed strange (doesn't Kaka pay Nidhi's tuition directly?) but you were too busy panicking about your own problem to think about it.

**Keep this to yourself until Round 3.** (You just... forgot about it in all the chaos. It'll come back to you.)

---

## 📣 What to Say & When

### Round 1
- **Your alibi:** "I went to wish Kaka goodnight around 9:15, then I watched cricket in the living room until the power went out."
- If someone brings up the shouting Rekha-bai heard, admit **"Fine, we argued."** Claim it was about something silly — cricket, politics, the proper way to make chai. Improvise!
- Do NOT mention the loan or the food truck.

### Round 2
- When the **will** is read and it mentions your loan... the jig is up. **Confess the $40,000 and Nitin's Naughty Naan** to the family (especially Minal). Be dramatic. Beg forgiveness.
- Point out: *"Why would I kill him? He was about to FORGIVE my loan!"* (Though... you didn't know that last night.)

### Round 3
- **You MUST reveal the Westbrook bursar letter** you saw on the desk. Say exactly what it said.

## 🧠 Tips
- You're a big red herring. You argued with the victim and shouted "You'll ruin me!" an hour before he died. Everyone will look at you. Stay calm (or don't — panicking is fun).
- Ask good questions: Where was everyone between 10:00 and 10:20 when the milk was made? Who had access to the kitchen?
- Remember: whoever the murderer is, they're allowed to lie. You aren't (except about your secret, until it's revealed).
