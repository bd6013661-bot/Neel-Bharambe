## 💀 Why You Did It
**At 8:30 PM**, after dinner, Kaka pulled you into the library. He had the bursar's letter. He knew *everything* — the money, KickFlip, the failing grade, all of it.

He said:
> *"At breakfast, I tell your parents everything. And my new will leaves you **nothing** until you tell them the truth yourself."*

You knew something he didn't think you knew: under Kaka's **old will** — the one that counts until he signs the new one — **you inherit Kesar Villa and most of his fortune.**

If Kaka didn't make it to breakfast, the new will would never be signed, the old will would stand, and your secret would die with him.

You panicked. And then you made a plan.

## 📣 What to Say & When

### Round 1
- **Your alibi:** "I was in my room FaceTiming my roommate Priya from about 9:40 to 10:50, then I showered."
- Act shocked and heartbroken. You were his favorite!
- Point at **Neel**: muddy sneaker prints? Kaka took his phone? A note that says "N—"? Very suspicious...
- Point at **Dad**: Rekha-bai heard a man shouting "You'll ruin me!"

### Round 2
- The will's line about "the truth" is smudged. Don't let anyone connect it to you. Suggest it's about Mom or Dad.
- If the chem notebook paper comes up: *"My backpack was downstairs in the living room all evening. Anyone could've torn a page out."* (It wasn't. But they don't know that.)
- Rekha-bai thinks the hooded figure was Neel. **Encourage that.** "He WAS wearing that hoodie all night..."
- Point at **Mom** too: she's a nurse, she handles the pills, and she was the last to see Kaka alive.

### Round 3
- You MUST show your real FaceTime log when asked (the clue card gives it). Explain the gap: *"The storm messed up the Wi-Fi, the call dropped."*
- If the camera shows a short hooded person: *"That could've been Mom!"* (Even though Mom was in a cardigan...)
- If you get cornered, you can crack and confess dramatically... **or** keep lying to the very end. Your choice. 😈

## 🧠 Tips
- Stay calm. Ask lots of questions — innocent people ask questions.
- Keep the pressure on Neel early, Dad in the middle, Mom at the end.
- Never admit you knew what was in the old will.
- **Win condition:** fewer than half the family accuses you at the end.
